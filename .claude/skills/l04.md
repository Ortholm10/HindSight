# L04 — Higher-timeframe merge without closure lag

**Looks like.** A daily strategy pulls in a weekly or 4-hour indicator merged on
a timestamp falling *inside* the higher-timeframe bar. The HTF value at row `t`
includes bars that had not closed at `t`. Inflation is modest and plausible —
Sharpe 0.8 to 1.6, not to absurdity. That plausibility is why it survives
review.

**Signals.**
- `.resample(...)` producing an HTF series later joined back to the base frame
- `pd.merge_asof(...)` where the right frame is coarser
- a join on a truncated timestamp key (`floor('D')`, `to_period('W')`)

...with no shift applied to the HTF series before the merge.

**Fix.** Shift the HTF series by one full HTF period before merging, so row `t`
sees only the last *closed* higher-timeframe bar. Equivalently, key the merge on
the HTF bar's close time rather than its open time.

**Not a leak.** Control C04: `merge_asof(direction='backward')` with the HTF
series pre-shifted. `direction='backward'` alone does **not** make a merge
safe — it finds the most recent HTF row at or before `t`, which is the
currently-open bar whose value already reflects data through `t`.

**Flagship.** Freqtrade lookahead-analysis has documented false negatives on
exactly this pattern (issues #11346, #12507).
