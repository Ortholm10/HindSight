# L10 — Non-chronological train/test split

**Looks like.** A supervised model over time-series features, split randomly
rather than chronologically. Test rows are interleaved with training rows
minutes apart and are near-duplicates; reported accuracy is wildly optimistic
and the strategy inherits the optimism.

**Signals.** `train_test_split(` without `shuffle=False`; `KFold`,
`cross_val_score`, or `GridSearchCV` without `TimeSeriesSplit` on a
time-indexed frame.

**Fix.** Chronological split, or `TimeSeriesSplit`.

**Not a leak.** Control C06 uses `TimeSeriesSplit` correctly.

**Scope.** Identical in demand forecasting and predictive maintenance — this is
the type that carries the "any time-series pipeline" framing.
