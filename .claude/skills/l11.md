# L11 — Preprocessing or feature selection fitted before the split

**Looks like.** A scaler, imputer, PCA, or feature selector fitted on the full
dataset, then the data is split. Test-set statistics are baked into the training
representation. Milder than [L10](l10.md), very common, rarely noticed.

**Signals.** `.fit(` or `.fit_transform(` on a frame subsequently passed to a
split function; `SelectKBest`, `RFE`, or correlation-based selection computed on
the full frame.

**Fix.** Fit inside the training fold only — in practice wrap in a `Pipeline`
and let the CV splitter handle it.

**Not a leak.** Control C06: scaler inside a `Pipeline` inside `TimeSeriesSplit`.
