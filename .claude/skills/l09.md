# L09 — Two-sided turning-point detection

**Looks like.** Peak or trough detection that compares a point to its neighbours
on *both* sides. A peak cannot be known until after it has passed; marking it at
its own timestamp is time travel. Effect is extreme.

**Signals.** `scipy.signal.argrelextrema`, `find_peaks`, `zigzag`, and any
hand-rolled comparison of `x[i]` against both `x[i-k]` and `x[i+k]`.

**Fix.** Causal confirmation — declare a peak only after `n` bars have failed to
exceed it, accepting the lag. If no causal equivalent exists, remove the column.

**Note.** Easy to detect once the function name is known, but one-shot LLM
baselines often accept it as legitimate technical analysis.
