# L05 — Full-sample normalisation

**Looks like.** A feature scaled by a statistic over the entire series. A
z-score of +2 in 2019 already knows the 2020-2024 distribution.

**Signals.**
- `(x - x.mean()) / x.std()` on a full column
- `.min()`, `.max()`, `.quantile()` on a full column feeding a threshold
- `StandardScaler().fit_transform(X)` / `MinMaxScaler()` on the full frame

**Fix.** `expanding().mean()` / `expanding().std()` (past-only, needs a warm-up),
or a trailing `rolling(n)`. Both are causal.

**Not a leak.** Control C02 uses `expanding()`; control C07 uses `.max()` for a
chart axis limit. `expanding()` is causal by construction and must never be
flagged.

**Note.** Inflation here is often modest, which makes L05 the best test of
continued suspicion: fix the loud leak first, then judge that the remaining
number is *still* implausible.
